01 / About

Curiosity, backed by implementation.

I'm a Software Engineer at Perfecter.ai and a Computer Engineering graduate of NIT Kurukshetra. I care about not just how to build AI systems, but why they work and where they fail.

It started with volunteering at an inclusive school, where my struggle to communicate in Indian Sign Language led me to build an ISL recognition tool. Later, my deepfake-detection study showed that strong results on familiar data rarely carried over to unseen datasets, which turned my focus to reliability.

Today I build video, document and retrieval systems at work, and software for the same school alongside. My long-term goal is reliable AI that makes learning and communication more accessible.

04 / Research experience

Research experience

Under review

Cross-Dataset Generalization in Deepfake Detection

Final-year project at NIT Kurukshetra, supervised by Dr. Ankit Kumar Jain.

  • Evaluated five pretrained vision models across FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF v2 and DFD.
  • Used controlled splits and video-level scoring to measure how much in-domain accuracy survives a change of dataset.
  • Every model degraded out of domain: the best in-domain AUC of 0.957 (ConvNeXt-Tiny) compares with 0.51–0.59 for all five models when trained on Celeb-DF and tested on FF++.
